About me

I'm Aya, a front-end developer who found my way into programming from a rather different field. I studied medical imaging and currently work as a radiology technician, but somewhere along the way, I discovered that I really enjoyed building things with code. What started as curiosity turned into months of teaching myself web development, experimenting with ideas, and building projects to understand how things actually work. These days, I work mainly with React, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS. I've built everything from small API-driven applications to SkillForge, a full-stack learning platform where I worked with authentication, Firestore, custom React hooks, and cloud-based image storage.

My Process

1- Explore

I start by understanding the problem I'm trying to solve, researching what I need, and breaking the idea into smaller pieces.

2- Plan

I sketch out the interface, user flow, and application structure before jumping into the code.

3- Build

This is where I turn the idea into a real application—building components, managing state, connecting APIs, and working with data.

4- Break & Improve

Things rarely work perfectly the first time. I debug, test, rethink what isn't working, and refine the application until it feels right.
